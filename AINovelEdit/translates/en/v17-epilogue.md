Henrietta began to move her fingers nervously through her hair as if she had lost her patience, but trying not to break her dignity as queen, she went to Saito and showed a smile.

"You've heard Saito-dono, we have the permission from your master, so there is no longer any obstacle, however, because of all the commotion that occurred recently, I think I would be in trouble to fall asleep, if it doesn't bother you please be so kind to accompany me with a glass of wine."

Caught between the hostilities of these two women, Saito did not know that it was appropriate to answer each proposition. He were entirely lost within himself helplessly. Truly, I was like walking among rows of barrels of gunpowder while holding a torch, if I made the slightest slip, Baaam! Think of a giant explosion.

Then in a voice trembling with anger, Louise said:

"No matter how many years pass Princes never change, you're just like when we were kids. When I was playing with one of my dolls "Ah Louise! That was a nice doll you have there, give it to me please!" and then I had to put a face as if nothing was wrong and I had to give it to you."

With a completely altered voice of almost shouting Henrietta replied:

"How dare you compare both men and dolls! But that is what you always do Louise."

These two were about to explode in a brilliant fireworks.

"I, I'll sleep outside, yes, so the princess can stay in her room and Louise can use my tent."

"I'm afraid I cannot let you do that, if I remember correctly I gave you an order to be my escort and thus you need to be responsible for my safety at all times, yes, yes, even if you have to sleep with me in the same bed."

Then, inevitably Louise exploded, unable to hold and shake her shoulders, as Louise came and brokenly murmured something.

"... Oh God, in truth ... since when did you became an adult?"

Henrietta slowly turned her gaze to Louise's face, her expression had become totally expressionless. Saito remembered seeing a face like this when they were in the royal palace. This expression on her face always emerged in critical situations, Saito could practically smell fuses being burned.

"Did you say something!!?"

"I said I only succeeded in revealing this useless adult sensuality of yours, your majesty!" Henrietta started shaking out of anger.

"You, perhaps fail to understand the consequences of what you're saying!"

"If only your ability to handle political affairs was as big as your sensuality, then we would not have to worry about the security of the country as we do now...!!"

After losing his consciousness, the fierce battle of these two women continue indefinitely, until at some point, the two gasped each other while both were dropped rendered on the bed. Still breathing hard ... without really having the strength to move yet, they said:

"Take care of Saito-dono. Bring him to your tent, it's an order."

"I ask you, do it as a favor, right now I want to be alone. " Henrietta said with a sad solemn voice.

Regaining consciousness, Saito realized that he was in his private tent. At the side of his bed, sitting on a folding chair Louise could be seen distracted while looking at the landscape from the window of the tent.

Saito suddenly felt something warm flowing through the inner edge of her eye.

Her figure was simply an appearance of divine beauty, dressed simply like a nun, but even this could not hide the incredible charm that radiated from Louise.

As her resolution was unshaken, Louise's eyes just turned gently to see the outside from that window, her mouth in a straight line only accentuated the serenity that now dwelt in her face. The charm possessed by Henrietta was so vast that you could be drown in it, indeed was disturbingly fascinating, however ...

If the beauty of Henrietta represents the evils of desire, then in contrast, the charm of Louise represents the "divine" blessing. This blessing was truly a sight quite unusual, but Saito really admired it... Like when she was determined to do something, like when she gathered the courage to face her fears, it was on those occasions when Saito was lucky enough to see Louise radiates this divine charm.

Charming women are very common in this world.

But only one women could show this divine expression, it was Louise.

Then in a voice that seemed stuck in his throat Saito said:

"Louise..."

Upon hearing this, Louise turned her head towards him.

"Hey, are you crying?"

"No ... Is that ... I'm so glad you're back ..."

Saito had realized that simple truth.

The reason I had decided to stay in this world.

Because Louise is here. There was no other reason. Then Saito also discovered the reason why I was attracted to Louise.

So far his life had always been gray and boring. When I lived in Tokyo, I had accepted this truth so naturally I have no time to questioned whether this was correct or not.

But the moment he met Louise, he learned a lot of things that until now he didn't knew to exist.

Fun stuff, happy things, sad things, painful things ...

The first time he went to a dance ... At the same time when I thought "how cute she is" his heart trembled for the first time, Louise had made his heart dance. And likewise, at the time he was in this world, Saito keeps thinking that there could be a "future" for him.

"I get the impression that Louise has transported me somewhere."

To a place not here, a very exciting place, I was transported to a fantasy world ...

Maybe this was only due to my imagination, maybe it was just a misunderstanding, but at some point I actually felt it, it sensed that this moment actually existed. And this became the most precious thing he had ever had in his life.

Seriously, what's the matter with this guy?

I love you, I could not take it anymore, I love you so much.
