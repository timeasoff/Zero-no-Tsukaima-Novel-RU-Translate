## Абзац 78 — RU пассив «был + причастие»: проверь агента (RGC: охвачена)

- **EN:** The village was in a state of chaos. In the empty center of the village, the young man held a staff in his hands, forcing Brimir to gather in the center. As Sasha, and Saito, who was standing on the edge, made their way there, Brimir gave them instructions.
